The stated symmetry result

Dependence on one coordinate

For each of their respective ranges, the manuscripts state that every solution satisfying the hypotheses has the form of an increasing one-dimensional layer.

u(x) = qs(e · x − a)

e ∈ S³, e₄ > 0, a ∈ ℝ

  • Bounded, entire, classical, and strictly monotoneThe equation holds throughout ℝ⁴, with ∂₄u > 0. No limits as x₄ → ±∞ are prescribed.
  • A normalized one-dimensional layerqs is increasing, qs(0) = 0, and qs(t) → ±1 as t → ±∞. The fractional Laplacian uses the Fourier symbol |ξ|2s.
  • Two open intervalsThe main statements cover 0 < s < 1/2 and 1/2 < s < 1. They do not include s = 1/2.
